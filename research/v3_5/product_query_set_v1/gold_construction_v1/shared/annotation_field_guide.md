# P7 Annotation Field Guide

Use the three P6 schemas as separate records. Retrieval Gold is bounded pooled
judgment and outside-pool returns remain unjudged. Evidence Gold uses only
official subtitle or ASR authority; navigation metadata is not evidence.
Alternative groups are OR and required spans within a set/group are AND.
Sufficiency uses exactly sufficient, partial, insufficient, or unverifiable.

Template placeholders and empty arrays mean “not started”; they are not Gold
decisions. Preserve query identity and source text/language. Do not overwrite
templates or earlier role outputs. Downstream failure codes are never Gold.
